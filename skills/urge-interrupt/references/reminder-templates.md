# Reminder Templates — Reference

Reminder tone is configurable: `supportive` | `direct` (default) | `clinical`

---

## Morning Check-in

**direct:**
> Morning. How did last night go?

> New day. Any high-risk windows coming up today — evening alone, stressful meeting, long downtime?

> Quick one: how are you starting today? Rate your baseline urge level 1–10.

**supportive:**
> Good morning. Checking in — how are you feeling heading into today?

> Morning. You've got a fresh start. Any situations today you want to think through in advance?

**clinical:**
> Morning check-in. Report last night's status and today's anticipated risk windows.

---

## Evening / Pre-High-Risk Window

**direct:**
> You're heading into the window. What's your plan for the next two hours?

> Pre-window check. How tired are you right now? What's going to be on your phone tonight?

> Evening prompt: stable, a little off, or already drifting?

**supportive:**
> Evening check-in. The next few hours can be high-risk. What are you doing to set yourself up?

> How's your energy right now? Tired evenings are high-risk. What's your plan?

**clinical:**
> Pre-window assessment. Current state? Environment controls in place?

---

## Weekly Review

**direct:**
> Week checkpoint. Streak count? What was the hardest moment and what did you do?

> Let's look at the week. What patterns did you notice? Any triggers repeat?

> You made it through the week. What's one thing to adjust going into next week?

**supportive:**
> End of week. You showed up all seven days — that matters. What felt hardest, and what helped?

> Weekly check-in. Reflect on the patterns you noticed. What do you want to carry into next week?

**clinical:**
> Weekly review. Record streak count, relapse events if any, trigger patterns observed, and one behavioral adjustment for next week.

---

## Post-Clean-Day

**direct:**
> You got through today. What helped?

> Clean day. What was the moment you were most tempted, if any?

**supportive:**
> That's another day. You did it. What made today work?

**clinical:**
> Day complete. Log: peak urge intensity (1–10), intervention used, outcome.
